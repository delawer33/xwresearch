## Question

"Competitor parity" needs a measuring stick. Which Saudi incumbent do we benchmark against —
Syarah, OpenSooq, YallaMotor, Motory — and on what dimensions (live-listing volume & freshness,
pricing/valuation feature, catalog depth, search/filter UX, seller-contact loop, trust signals)?

Produce a parity scorecard: for each benchmark, where mawtarx leads, matches, and trails today,
with the ~3 dimensions that most define "working" in the Saudi market. Feeds the coverage target
(004), pricing-trust bar (003), and catalog-readiness bar (010).

Resolve to: chosen benchmark(s) + a scorecard + the parity dimensions that gate "working".

## Resolution (2026-07-30, research)

**Benchmarks:** **Motory** = direct structural peer (aggregated listings + valuation tool +
editorial + dealer-contact, no reconditioning — the shape mawtarx is actually building). **Syarah**
= trust ceiling (Elm-backed transactional retailer: 200-pt inspection, 1-yr warranty, 10-day
return — the credibility bar, but a model mawtarx can't copy since it aggregates, doesn't own
inventory). **Haraj + OpenSooq** = liquidity floor (Haraj ~12.4M SA visits/mo is the whole game;
WAF-locked → 0 ingestible).

**The 3 dimensions that define "working" in SA used-cars:** (1) **liquidity** — live volume &
freshness (mawtarx's built journey is edge-gated/not publicly reachable = the fatal gap for a
*consumer* surface; less binding for the API-first path); (2) **transaction-trust** — Syarah's
inspection/warranty/return moat (mawtarx has none, structurally); (3) a **reachable buyer↔seller
contact loop** (mawtarx = reveal-phone only, no on-platform lead).

**Key strategic read:** valuation/pricing is now **table-stakes** (3 of 4 incumbents ship it), so
mawtarx's genuinely-better honest engine is a *latent* advantage — it only counts once **shipped +
un-stale** (ties directly to 003 + 008/013). Feeds 004 (target ≈ OpenSooq-scale live SA inventory),
010 (depth must reach Motory/YallaMotor spec-level, not just breadth).

_Caveat: incumbent live-listing counts are marketing-stated (sites WAF-blocked); traffic figures
are SEMrush/Ahrefs (solid). Full source list in the ticket's research transcript._
